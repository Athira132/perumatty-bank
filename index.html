Welcome to Perumatty Service Co-operative Bank

On the Path of Development with the Country...

Perumatti Service Cooperative Bank is becoming the heartbeat of the country in Perumatti, which is synonymous with agricultural prosperity and is bordered by Tamil Nadu to the south and Shokanashini River to the north in Chittoor, Palakkad district.

The bank's progress is based on a tradition of seven and a half decades. The bank has made strong interventions in all sectors including education, social welfare, agriculture, philanthropy, and women's empowerment.

The fact that it has been continuously operating in profit since its inception in 1949 adds to the success of the bank. This cooperative institution, which operates in Class A classification with 31,412 members, is at the forefront of the leading cooperative institutions in the district.

Agricultural Development

Empowering Farmers with Modern Technologies

The bank, which was the first in Kerala to implement micro-agriculture, has achieved great progress in the field of agriculture. The demonstration farm started in Kampalathara in 2007 successfully cultivated various vegetable crops.

To implement precision farming according to Israeli technology, dealership of leading companies in this field was established. Under this program, drip irrigation materials worth Rs. 8.61 crore were distributed to 6,641 farmers in 2,400 hectares of land, accompanied by full technical assistance.

Capital loans were provided to Perumatti Agropros and Perumatti Agroservice Center to spread and promote scientific farming methods. A Farmers Service Center functions under the bank, helping small farmers by renting tractors, pickup vans, and garden tractors at highly subsidized rates.
