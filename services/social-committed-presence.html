Agricultural Interventions

Empowering the Farmers of Perumatty

Perumatty is synonymous with agricultural prosperity, and the bank acts as its financial spine. We were the first in Kerala to implement micro-agriculture, starting a model demonstration farm in Kampalathara in 2007.

From Israeli drip irrigation partnerships that distributed Rs. 8.61 crore of materials to 6,641 farmers, to providing Rs. 52 crore interest-free loans to paddy farmers and financing Agro-Service Centers, we work closely to secure the livelihood of our primary producers.

Social Welfare & Education

Supporting Education and Social Security

We believe that the development of a country starts with its children and youth. The bank actively intervenes in the education sector of Palakkad district, providing financial aid, scholarships, and supporting local schools to ensure quality infrastructure.

Welfare schemes, medical aid support, and cooperative charity services are regularly extended to economically weaker sections of the community, building a strong local safety net.

Welfare & Empowerment

Women Empowerment & Philanthropy

Women's empowerment is a vital area of our social intervention. The bank supports various self-help groups (SHGs) and neighborhood networks, providing them with subsidized capital, skill training, and credit facilities to set up home-based micro units, dairy units, and handicraft works.

Our philanthropic efforts extend beyond banking transactions. During times of medical distress, natural crises, or personal emergencies, Perumatty Service Cooperative Bank has consistently stood at the forefront to offer immediate, direct financial relief and voluntary support to the common people.
